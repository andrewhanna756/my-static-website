Hobbies

Drawing

I used to spend hours after school drawing comic strips about my pet dog going on made-up adventures.

My favourite memory is when my teacher pinned one of my drawings to the classroom wall for the whole term.

Cycling

Every weekend I rode my bike around the neighbourhood with friends until the streetlights came on.

I still remember the thrill of finally learning to ride without training wheels in our driveway.

Toys

Lego Bricks

I had a huge box of Lego that I would tip out onto the carpet and build spaceships from for hours.

One time I built a tower so tall it toppled over, and my little brother thought it was hilarious.

Teddy Bear

My teddy bear, named Buttons, went with me everywhere — even to the dinner table.

I was devastated the day I lost him at the park, but my dad found him under a bench an hour later.
